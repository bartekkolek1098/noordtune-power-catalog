## Special exclusion and workshop review rules

- **Qashqai J11 1.2 DIG-T**: manual/CVT/X-Tronic rated torque and ECU are unknown from RDW. No swap with 1.3 DIG-T or J12.
- **Transit Connect EcoBlue**: 73.3, 73.5 and 88.3 kW factory RDW cohorts are kept separate; installed 1.5 TDCi EU5 vs EcoBlue, ECU updates and original stock torque conflict must be resolved.
- **BMW 320i F30**: 1997 cc N20 and 1998 cc B48 separately scoped; 2015 transition demands vehicle engine/ECU scan. Conservative B48 BR+Mosselman results used; Shiftech 300PS high-power indication intentionally not adopted.
- **VAG diesels / Caddy / Crafter / Transporter**: factory de-rating and gearbox/DSG limits must be reviewed, especially published 400–520Nm Stage 1. 2024 Crafter revised ECU remains withheld.
- **Polo 6R, Golf VII, Tiguan II**: precise RDW factory kW and admission years prevent 6C, GTI 230/245, Tiguan I/II and later mild-hybrid cross-matches.
- **Renault 1.2 TCe / Master**: phase, factory kW, emissions equipment and engine family matter. The 2017 dCi and 2022 Blue dCi are different reviewed cases.
- **Known unresolved engines remain withheld**: 2024 Transit Connect revision, hybrid/mild-hybrid without fuel-/generation-specific evidence, 2019–2021 Peugeot 508 1.6 non-hybrid lacking robust two-source corroboration, and other atmospheric/ECU-ambiguous variants.

## Reproducible QA and privacy

- Source and identity metadata live in `src/data/reviewed-rdw-bulk-batch-4.ts`; archived source profiles in `src/data/tuning-profiles/`.
- `pnpm test:rdw:focused` includes all four packages; fourth-batch negatives cover 15 independent wrong-scope perturbations per app (**375**) and 115 exact frozen positive matches.
- `node --no-warnings scripts/report-rdw-evidence-funnel.ts --check` must reproduce frozen sample aggregates without plate/VIN/raw registration logging.
- Customer browser acceptance uses `scripts/qa-rdw-bulk-4-browser.cjs` via the combined risk-based fast gate; every one of 25 new engines has a real RDW mock browser journey and previous batches have deterministic regressions.
- Release only after typecheck/build, exact-commit preview and protected preview/browser acceptance, followed by verified deployment. Do not infer a share of the complete Dutch fleet from this frozen 3,000-case test.
