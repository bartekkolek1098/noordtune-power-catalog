# NoordTune NL van photography licences — 2026-10-13

Every asset in `public/images/seo-vans` is an **illustrative photograph of the van model/generation**, not a NoordTune customer vehicle nor a demonstration of the engine under the bonnet. Licences are reviewed and creators credited visibly per image on the public site.

All photos are retrieved through Wikimedia Commons from specific **manually selected** file titles and are resized thumbnail derivatives. Source, author, licence and direct licensing URL are recorded in `src/data/nl-van-generation-photos.json` with SHA256 for integrity checking.
